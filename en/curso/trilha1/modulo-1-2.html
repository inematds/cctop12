🎙️ Voice Mode

O /voice opens full spoken conversations with Claude— hands-free. It works for both chatting and dictate. Great for brainstorming without a keyboard; external tools like Wispr Flow make dictation even faster.

💡 Practical tip

Use voice for the think out loud — describing a long problem is faster by speaking than typing. For high-speed dictation, you can pair it with an external tool like Wispr Flow.

🖥️ Cowork

Cowork is the desktop agent WITHOUT a terminal from Anthropic, built for knowledge work. It’s a great product — but almost everything it does can be done in Claude Code, with greater capacity. Its place is as entry point for non-technical people who are overwhelmed.

✓ When Cowork shines

  • ✓A nontechnical person who’s intimidated by the terminal.
  • ✓Anyone who’s overwhelmed and wants a simple entry point.
  • ✓Knowledge work without needing everything in Code.

✗ Where Code wins

  • ✗Almost everything in Cowork also runs in Claude Code.
  • ✗Code has more capability and more control.
  • ✗For advanced use, the terminal is still home.

💡 Practical tip

If you're already comfortable in the terminal, start with Code. Save Cowork for introducing the ecosystem to someone who isn’t technical — it’s the gentlest way to show what an agent does.

📁 Local File Access

Connect a folder for Claude read and organize your files. It's one of the major benefits of local terminal agents: they don't just talk about the files—they actually work with them, reading, organizing, and editing.

🗂️ What it does with the folder

  • •Reads the content to understand what's there.
  • •Organizes, renames, and restructures when you ask.
  • •Edits files in place — it’s not read-only.

💡 Practical tip

Point it to a messy downloads or notes folder and ask it to organize things. It's the fastest way to see why having the agent in your local files is so valuable.

📚 Citations & Chat Search

Two anchoring and recovery features. Citations grounds responses in your documents with exact citations (via API). Chat Search searches all your past conversations — retrieves what you’ve already discussed.

✓ Citations

  • ✓Grounds the response in your documents.
  • ✓Shows the exact citation for where each excerpt came from.
  • ✓Available via API: trust and traceability.

✓ Chat Search

  • ✓Searches all past conversations.
  • ✓Recovers that decision you made weeks ago.
  • ✓"Where have we talked about this before?" — solved.

💡 Practical tip

Think of them as a pair: Citations says where the answer came from; Chat Search says where you’ve already talked about it. Together, they turn history and documents into useful memory.

💬 Web & collaboration

The layer that brings Claude wherever your team already works — and gives you privacy when you need it: Claude in Slack, Incognito Chats, /teleport, Cowork Plugins e Cowork Projects.

Collaboration

  • •Claude in Slack — mention @Claude and it does the task and replies in the channel.
  • •Cowork Plugins — extends Cowork with extra capabilities.
  • •Cowork Projects — organizes work by project in Cowork.

Privacy & bridge

  • •Incognito Chats — chat that isn't saved, remembered, or used for training.
  • •/teleport — pulls a Claude web session into the terminal.

💡 Practical tip

Start something casually on your phone or the web, and when you want the power of the terminal, use /teleport to bring the session to Code without losing context.
